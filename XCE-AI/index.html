Deployment

Why local-first matters.

  • Large project context — assets, scenes, scripts, textures, audio and logs
  • High-frequency iteration — every tweak shouldn't require costly cloud calls
  • Privacy and commercial IP — unreleased scripts and brand assets stay local
  • Offline creation — Steam users, university labs and small-team workstations

Lite Local

Creators & university teaching

Small models, quantized models, local STT/TTS and lightweight image generation.

Pro Workstation

Indie teams & professional creators

RTX 4090/5090 or multi-GPU setups running full LLM, image, video and 3D generation.

Studio / Enterprise

Enterprises, universities & studios

Private GPU servers, multi-user queues, permissions, asset libraries and audit trails.
